# Module 10 Damage Image Sourcing Guide

## 📸 Required Images

### Set 1: Clear Hail Damage (3-5 images)
**Purpose**: Challenge 1 - Easy difficulty, obvious damage

**What to look for:**
- Clear circular impact marks on shingles
- Multiple visible dents or divots
- Obvious granule loss in circular patterns
- Close-up shots showing texture detail
- Well-lit, high contrast images

**Recommended Hotspots per Image**: 3 spots

---

### Set 2: Non-Qualifying/Mixed Damage (2-3 images)
**Purpose**: Challenge 2 & 3 - Medium/Hard difficulty

**What to look for:**
- Normal wear and tear (NOT hail damage)
- Aging shingles with general granule loss
- Wind damage, curling, or lifting
- Manufacturing defects
- Mix of minor impacts with normal wear

**Recommended Hotspots per Image**: 3 spots (marking the few actual damage points)
