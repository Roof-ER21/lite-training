# Lite Training

## Register

product

## Users

Roof-ER sales reps learning and reviewing roofing, inspection, and sales material,
often on a phone outdoors. Managers review their training and assessment records.

## Product Purpose

Help reps learn the material, practice conversations, continue their training,
and understand their exam standing without losing saved work.

## Brand Personality

Practical, clear, and restrained. Use the Roof-ER identity established in the
September 12 design pickup, adapted for extended reading rather than marketing.

## Anti-references

Arcade scoreboards, streaks, XP, confetti, decorative emoji, pastel gradient
callouts, and generic marketing claims. No training clock or completion percentages.
Exam scores remain assessments and must remain visible.

## Design Principles

1. Preserve rep progress, signatures, certification records, and the Continue action.
2. Support both white/light and dark mode, as Ahmed explicitly requested September 12.
3. Keep useful training content and interaction behavior ahead of decoration.
4. Preserve the distinction between AI grading and degraded keyword grading.
5. Defer security changes per Ahmed's September 12 instruction. Do not deploy or push.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA, readable text outdoors, visible keyboard focus, semantic
controls, and reduced motion. Preserve saved theme preferences and the existing
system-theme default. Insurance statements need Reese/legal review.
